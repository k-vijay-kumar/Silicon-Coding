# FPGA and ASIC Trade-offs

FPGAs provide reconfigurable logic and routing; ASICs use a technology-specific, fixed implementation.

- **FPGA:** Reprogrammability and shorter development cycles, with area and routing overhead from configurable resources.
- **ASIC:** Potentially smaller or lower-power implementations at volume, with non-recurring design cost and no post-fabrication reconfiguration.

These are broad trade-offs, not guarantees. Process, design, volume, and requirements matter.
